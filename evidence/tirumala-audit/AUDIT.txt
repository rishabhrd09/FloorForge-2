TIRUMALA 2 / TIRUMALA 3 — WALKTHROUGH AND GEOMETRY AUDIT
30 October 2026

Scope
Reviewed the supplied implementation transcript, the actual generated models,
the approved plan data, the shared viewer and the running browser experience.
Tirumala 2 uses P12-A p12-open-porch-v33. Tirumala 3 uses P11
p11-veranda-return-trim-v32. Both plots are 40 ft north–south by 50 ft east–west.
Existing unrelated workspace changes were retained.

Reproduced failure
Both samples started outside the plot on visible context geometry that had no
walking collision. After three seconds the player was about 11 metres below
the scene instead of standing on the road. The earlier sample-load/screenshot
checks did not detect this.

Changes completed
1. Ground and entry: added collision for the visible road, verge, kerb, lawn,
   court and distant ground; moved the arrival point to the pedestrian portion
   of the existing east gate. The visitor now stays supported and can walk into
   the plot. House/veranda/forecourt remain raised above the lower car porch.
2. Floors: filled a missing living-to-lobby floor patch and small obsolete
   furniture-mask holes. These were causing drops or raised-slab obstructions.
   Existing floors/walls are subtracted from the repairs to avoid overlapping
   coplanar surfaces. Room boundaries have not moved.
3. Doors: supplied explicit motion for complete leaves, frames and handles.
   ICU glass stacks outside its aperture; the store slider operates; hinges
   follow the approved swings. The living entrance has four wooden panels and
   an independently retractable mesh screen. F opens/closes targeted openings.
   Windows move their sashes and glass together. Initial open poses are also
   included in static exports, rather than only the interactive viewer.
4. Stairs: removed the roof obstruction over the upper flight/landing, added
   a raised glass rooflight and landing edge guards. Rooflight top is
   5.25–5.30 m above house datum, providing over 2.1 m headroom at the 3.15 m
   landing. This changes roof elevation locally; it does not move the stair
   footprint or change its 18 risers. Main room ceiling remains 3.508 m.
5. Views: interior cameras now update the walking controller as well as the
   camera, use 1.63 m eye height and remain grounded. Exterior views exit walking
   cleanly. The P11 ICU camera no longer starts partly inside its wall. Fields
   of view are 64–68 degrees; actual room scale is not enlarged.
6. Interface: restored usable canvas height instead of a shallow viewport;
   Walk in / Explore inside scroll the viewer into view. P11/P12 no longer show
   unrelated original Tirumala A–E alternatives or upper-floor camera controls.
   Ground-floor tips and project descriptions now match the supplied model.
7. Dimensions/specifications: replaced duplicated inaccurate prose with
   geometry-derived sizes. Corrected ICU polygon area, serving worktop,
   aperture versus jamb clearance, ceiling height, stair count and source hash.
   The validation report now checks actual geometry and connectivity instead
   of returning mostly fixed PASS labels.
8. Presentation/delivery: regenerated dimensioned 2D drawings, material views,
   curated 3D previews, specifications, design guides and export packages.
   Interior cameras use the live viewer's actual lighting/exposure settings.

Preserved design
Approved room/wall/opening coordinates, kitchen L-counter, stair layout and
furniture footprints remain fixed. No refrigerator or puja room was added.
P12 drawing room stays empty; its ICU retains only the north-facing recliner
(head south, feet north) and corner table. P11 retains its approved furniture.
Large cream/ivory tile finishes and the lower traditional-style porch remain.
Both source plan hashes remain unchanged:

P11: b0d3db66be33590db5172cecd6fd8d5c4a1a0a909a2faec6c3c7ba7bff2eb5f4
58 original sample files checked against the pre-audit snapshot are unchanged.
The original electrical-tour viewer remains at its protected baseline hash.

Dimensional findings
P12 ICU clear bounding span: 10 ft 1 3/4 in by 14 ft 11 in; actual polygon area
136.2 sq ft after the porch corner cut-out. P11 ICU: 11 ft 3 in by
11 ft 4 7/8 in; area 128.3 sq ft. Master bath remains 8 ft by 6 ft.
The 3 ft lobby, 2 ft 11 3/8 in store and existing door apertures remain relatively
narrow by design. Lighting and correct camera perspective cannot create extra
physical clearance there. This pass does not silently widen them.
P12 porch is stepped, not an unobstructed full 10 by 15 ft rectangle; the
straight entry depth is about 14 ft 3 1/4 in. Parking fit is vehicle-dependent.
The 6-inch porch drop is a provisional visualization parameter, not a separately
approved construction level. Full room dimensions are in dimensions.csv and
each project's DESIGN_GUIDE.md. Bounding spans of irregular rooms must not be
treated as rectangular room areas.

Full-suite qualification
The initial broad run reported 738 passed, 4 failed and 9 errors. Eleven cases
were prevented by sandbox loopback permissions and passed in the later server
batch. A temporary original-bundle preservation mismatch was restored to the
verified original baseline, and the preservation test then passed. One legacy
test, test_tour_page_loads_its_data_offline, still expects the frozen original
electrical-tour viewer to be byte-identical to the newer shared viewer. That
expectation conflicts with preserving the original package. It has not been
hidden or weakened. The entire historical suite is therefore not claimed green.

Rendering limit
These are interactive real-time raster/PBR previews, not path-traced photographs.
The changes improve navigability, readable proportions and presentation; they
do not certify construction, structural design or wheelchair/gurney access.

How to review
Reload the application and reopen Tirumala 2 or Tirumala 3 from the sample
gallery so the refreshed scene and viewer are loaded. Select Walk in; use
WASD or arrow keys to move and F on a targeted door/window to operate it.
Explore inside provides grounded kitchen/living and ICU/veranda viewpoints.
