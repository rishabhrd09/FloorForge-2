Tirumala 2 retains finalized P12-A (p12-open-porch-v33).
Tirumala 3 retains finalized P11 (p11-veranda-return-trim-v32).

REQUESTS APPLIED
- Both remain 40 ft north–south × 50 ft east–west. Every approved ground-room
  polygon, wall footprint, opening position, kitchen counter, serving worktop,
  furniture footprint and gate position is preserved. Spaciousness is improved
  through height, connected views, lighting and finishes, not hidden rescaling.
- Warm ivory walls, cream 24 × 48 in marble-look floor tiles, limestone accents,
  teak joinery/soffits and charcoal frames reference My Desired Home.
- The hall-to-ICU entrance has two independently operable layers: parallel
  sliding glass and wooden privacy panels. Both clear the original aperture.
  The separate veranda-side glass opening retains its approved side stack.
- The veranda has a timber ceiling/entry beam and warm light. The north strip
  has grass, low planting and stepping pads within its existing two-foot width.
- The Kota-style lower car porch with red-oxide border remains 6 inches below
  the house/veranda datum. This level difference remains a provisional design.
- P12 keeps the drawing room unfurnished, the connected kitchen/living/dining
  space open, and only the approved ICU recliner plus corner table. No fridge.
- Added a complete first floor with a master suite above the ground master,
  office, guest bedroom, master ensuite, shared bathroom, gallery and terrace.
- The gallery overlooks a real 5.05 m × 2.925 m opening above living/dining;
  its clear height is 6.991 m (about 23 ft). The lightwell remains open above.
- The first-floor open terrace has a pergola, lounge, planting and glass guards.
  Access is directly from the gallery, not a stair to the roof above floor one.
- Ground stair geometry retains its original 18 risers to +3.150 m. Two new
  179 mm arrival risers connect to the upper floor at +3.508 m.
- Added upper-floor room cameras and a double-height living camera. They enter
  walking mode at actual standing eye height and remain at the correct level.

plans are also included in each sample's downloadable ZIP.

LIMITS
These are real-time rendered design models, not path-traced photographs or
construction documents. Ground room sizes were deliberately retained as
requested. An architect/engineer still needs to design structural support,
services, stair compliance and local approvals before construction.

This revision used targeted tests, not a fresh complete repository suite. The
prior audit records a legacy electrical-tour bundle-equality test conflict:
that frozen original sample is preserved rather than overwritten to match the
current shared viewer bundle. See evidence/tirumala-audit/AUDIT.txt.
