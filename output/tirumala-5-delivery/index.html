Tirumala 5Three floors, connected from inside.

The upper stairs now sit directly above the ground-floor stairs, reached by an indoor gallery beside the east glazing. The north bedroom extends one foot south at its front, with the rear passage retained. Ivory surfaces, large stone-look slabs and warm teak bring the three floors together.

The 40 × 50 ft plot, ground floor, second-floor hall and large first-floor terrace are retained. Hanging gold-disc decorations have been removed.

Implemented revision

  • Stacked indoor stairs: first-floor arrival → east-side gallery → south starter → west flight → north flight → enclosed second landing → hall.

  • ICU: recliner head south, feet north, with a 3 ft clear foot aisle. Compact chair repositioned.
  • Utility: 1.8 m ivory south privacy wall and ventilated teak east screen. North-side access stays open.
  • Clean stairwell: the three hanging gold-disc groups are removed. Ceiling lighting remains.

Reference and material notes

The supplied videos and images informed the ivory palette, tall glazing, timber doors and soffits, large floor slabs, gallery character and terrace planting. The exterior reel carries an AI-content label, so its appearance was treated as inspiration rather than construction evidence. Room sizes were taken from the project geometry.

  • Laminam slab formats informed the large-format surface appearance. The 3.2 × 1.6 m rendering module is a finish concept, not a product order.
  • Schüco sliding systems informed the large-glass, slim-sightline appearance while retaining visible frames and tracks.
  • IIT Kanpur / NICEE: load paths informed the architectural coordination of continuous support lines.
  • WBDG: vegetative roof guidance informed the notes on roof planting, drainage and structural loading.
