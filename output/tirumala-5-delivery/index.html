Tirumala 5A white gallery, connected by daylight.

The first-floor gallery now has a continuous solid warm-white parapet with ivory coping, including the long edge beside the bridge. Both stacked stairs use matching charcoal vertical pickets and teak handrails. The second-floor guard retains its metal-and-teak finish. A glazed rooflight brings daylight through the aligned atrium. Tall east-facing glazing, a wider north-facing drawing-room window and restrained ivory, limestone and teak finishes keep the house bright and calm.

The 40 × 50 ft plot, bedrooms, kitchen, ICU, family hall and large first-floor terrace retain their dimensions. The shortened second-floor landing has 124.3 sq ft of clear floor, with 477.2 sq ft of open roof garden retained. Four compact solar modules sit above the hall.

Passage end · before → after

Before: the enclosed east passage continued into an unused window-side tail, with 135.9 sq ft of landing/gallery floor. After: that tail is removed, leaving 124.3 sq ft, and a continuous visible metal-picket guard with a teak handrail closes the shortened end and joins the atrium edge. This 11.6 sq ft trim is confined to the second floor; stairs, ground and first floors, terraces, highest opaque roof and atrium/skylight retain their geometry.

Current revision

  • First-floor gallery: solid warm-white parapet with flush ivory coping, 120 mm thick and 1100 mm high. The long bridge edge now joins the L-shaped wall with the same opaque finish and coping.
  • Matching stair railings: charcoal vertical pickets and teak handrails on both ground-to-first and first-to-second flights, including the arrival steps. Stair geometry and floor openings stay fixed.
  • Continuous daylight: a 3325 × 1725 mm clear aperture reaches the glazed rooflight through the second-floor slab and highest roof.
  • Shortened east gallery: 124.3 sq ft of clear landing area, 37.2 sq ft above the earlier stacked-gallery layout. A continuous metal-and-teak guard closes the new end and follows the atrium edge; 477.2 sq ft of open second-floor terrace remains.
  • Glazing: south-facing upper windows are removed; tall east bays and a single 7′10½″ drawing-room north window take their place.
  • Roof equipment: four compact modules above the hall, separate from the atrium and terrace circulation.
  • Stacked indoor stairs: first-floor arrival → east-side gallery → south starter → west flight → north flight → enclosed second landing → hall.
  • North bedroom enlarged by 9.4 sq ft. Front width 11′1⅜″, rear width 10′1⅜″ and length 16′6⅞″. The stepped shape preserves the 3 ft rear passage.
  • Indoor gallery: 3′6½″ clear walking width, with a guarded double-height opening beside it.
  • ICU: recliner head south, feet north, with a 3 ft clear foot aisle. Compact chair repositioned.
  • Utility: 1.8 m ivory south privacy wall and ventilated teak east screen. North-side access stays open.
  • Clean stairwell: the three hanging gold-disc groups are removed. Ceiling lighting remains.

Reference and material notes

The supplied videos and images informed the ivory palette, tall glazing, timber doors and soffits, large floor slabs, gallery character and terrace planting. The exterior reel carries an AI-content label, so its appearance was treated as inspiration rather than construction evidence. Room sizes were taken from the project geometry.

  • Laminam slab formats informed the large-format surface appearance. The 3.2 × 1.6 m rendering module is a finish concept, not a product order.
  • Schüco sliding systems informed the large-glass, slim-sightline appearance while retaining visible frames and tracks.
  • IIT Kanpur / NICEE: load paths informed the architectural coordination of continuous support lines.
  • WBDG: vegetative roof guidance informed the notes on roof planting, drainage and structural loading.
