TIRUMALA 4 — EXTERIOR REFINEMENT, 31 OCTOBER 2026

Applied to the existing Tirumala 4 sample based on Tirumala 3 / P13.

What the review found
- The small red projecting elements were solid burgundy boxes used as hedge proxies.
- Small white flowers were also represented by boxes in the colony planting.
- A gap at the north/east road corner appeared as an accidental grass patch.
- Large paver modules, heavy rectangular streetlights and cool lighting weakened the exterior appearance.
- Tall ornamental grass spilled visually over the pads in the narrow north-side strip.

Implemented
- Removed the burgundy and white proxy boxes; replaced them with low leaf geometry and small flowers.
- Refined the house boundary using ivory render, a limestone base, thin coping, fine joints and small inset lights.
- Preserved the photographed colony wall character separately in muted mauve-grey.
- Added narrow soil beds and flush stone edges under the existing hedge runs.
- Paved the empty corner around a defined grass/groundcover pocket, keeping the gate clear.
- Shortened lawn blades and reduced ornamental accents in the existing north-side strip; retained its stepping pads.
- Added textured concrete road and smaller paver modules; moved the north drain covers to the verge's road edge.
- Replaced chunky streetlight beams with slimmer round poles at their existing positions.
- Applied Tirumala-4-only warm-neutral bounce lighting and a softer overcast option.
- Refreshed the gallery thumbnail, render set, standalone preview, model exports and export ZIP.
- Corrected several source-photo filename labels in the comparison sheet.

Preserved
- 40 ft north-south by 50 ft east-west plot.
- Entire building.json, including room polygons, dimensions, walls, openings, staircase, furniture layout, G+1 layout and levels.
- Gate opening and existing house circulation.
- Tirumala 2, Tirumala 3, original Tirumala, My Desired Home and Bungalow sample files.
- Ground + upper geometry hash: b6484526b3006577c8ea77d195487ec31e7226a90a9804cd2f2d400bc74b78fc

Verification
- 32 Python sample/plan tests and 87 viewer/walking tests passed.
- Browser checks cover the sample gallery, door layers, exterior materials, all capture cameras, optional parking view and porch/ground/upper-floor walking levels.
- Complete file and geometry comparison, browser-check count and export integrity are recorded in verification.json.

Scope of realism
The images are screenshots of the actual interactive model. Planting, materials and light have been improved; they are not photographs. Neighbour buildings and trees remain simplified, and unmeasured colony positions are approximate. The changes do not enlarge rooms or imply a surveyed site reconstruction.
