MY DESIRED HOME — 40 × 50 CONCEPT PACKAGE

Four independent proposals based on the ORIGINAL 55 × 60 My Desired Home.
Original projects and the sample gallery are unchanged. This is a review package,
not a registered FloorForge sample or completed 3D model.

A — Balanced care enlargement (recommended starting point).
B — Exact original care-room dimensions; larger drawing/living areas.
C — Largest care room; smaller drawing room.
D — Original care rectangle rotated; north path; no upper living void.

Each option includes ground and first-floor drawings. Original reference plans
and Tirumala context are included. All drawings are north-up.
The shared exterior context places garden/12 m road north and court/9 m road east.
These areas are outside the private 2,000 sq ft plot.

implementation-prompt.txt is the complete reusable prompt.
concepts.json contains exact concept geometry in feet.
room-schedule.csv lists rooms, dimensions and areas.
validation.json records plot, overlap, door and connection checks.
source-integrity.json verifies source files were unchanged.

Concept only: setbacks, boundary openings, structure, ventilation, stair headroom,
usable care access and equipment clearances need design review.
