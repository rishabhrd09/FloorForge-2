# Design your home

Choose **Sample projects · explore in 3D** at the top of the left sidebar. The gallery includes the saved Verandah House, the original startup home, Garden Pavilion, Quiet Studio and Palm Terrace Villa. Each card shows a real render plus plot size, bedrooms and occupied floors. **Explore in 3D** opens a sample without changing your project; choose **Dollhouse**, switch floors or **Walk in**. **Back to my project** closes the preview. **Use this sample** opens its matching project and model for editing; **Back to my previous project** restores your prior work, even after refreshing. Browsing never generates a replacement for your current design.

For a quick change to the default example, choose **Home-screen settings · no drawing**, edit the fields and click **Generate my home**. **Edit the design shown** fills those fields from the visible house and switches off saved sketches, guides, descriptions and placement overrides. Your saved sketches remain available. Choose **Settings + my floor guide** or **My room arrangement** only when you want that input applied. Saved room/furniture moves have their own checkbox.

In Walk mode, drag to look around; the cursor stays free when you release the mouse. Arrow keys move forward/back and turn; A/D move sideways. Escape pauses walking. **Fullscreen**, **Virtual tour** and the other viewer actions stay in the toolbar above the view.

To arrange rooms yourself:

1. Choose **Design rooms**. Tap a room to add it, or select an empty spot first. There are eight easy placement spots per floor—left/right, front/rear—with no measurements to enter.
2. Drag a room to move it. Drop it onto another room to swap them. You can also tap one card and then another spot. **Undo** reverses a change.
3. Switch floor tabs to arrange upstairs. One staircase card stays linked on every floor; moving it moves the linked core and swaps affected rooms together. Outdoor spaces keep the area directly above open.
4. Choose **Build my layout**. The software fits room sizes, a connecting passage, walls, doors, windows and stairs. Review the connected layout, then choose **Yes, view in 3D** or **No, keep arranging**. Your existing drawing is retained until approval.
5. **More options** contains floor count, roof access, a sample arrangement, copying rooms from below, and optional detailed drawing tools. **G+1** stays two occupied floors; roof access adds stairs to the roof, not another occupied floor.
6. Save the project to continue later; the arrangement also autosaves on this device. **Edit placements** remains available after generation for supported furniture and room moves.

The simple board generates a home around a shared passage, with up to eight room cards per floor. It preserves the chosen side and front-to-rear ordering while fitting dimensions. If the site is too small, it explains the remaining space choice and keeps the arrangement. Detailed tools remain available for other geometries. Opening the simple board alone does not replace a saved detailed drawing.

A drawing room is a guest sitting room. Choose Living for a combined family/guest lounge. A drying room is a separate laundry/service space.

The same rendering materials, lighting, themes and camera controls are retained. Changing rooms changes the geometry. FloorForge remains a preliminary design tool; this update does not turn its plans into construction documents.

Use **Open your sketch example · G+1** on the home screen for the worked example based on the supplied ground-floor screenshot. It opens a matching generated model and editable room plan. Close the editor to compare Exterior, Dollhouse and Plan, or use Open floor guides for the saved positional overview. The original example remains available. The screenshot reconstruction and the fitted project are also saved separately under examples/custom.

On First or Second, the editor shows the footprint below, the blue stair connection and areas that must stay open to the sky. **Continue stairs from below** proposes the matching stair without adding an overlapping duplicate. **Suggest this floor from below** previews a layout derived from the lower rooms; Yes replaces only the selected upper floor and Undo restores it. Smart fit on an upper floor keeps other floors unchanged. A narrow lower staircase is identified before you continue it upstairs.

The worked example retains the ground-floor room arrangement, bedroom dimensions and drawing-room dimensions. Stair, dining, kitchen, living and veranda dimensions are adjusted as listed under **What we adjusted**. Coordinates are reconstructed from the screenshot, not recovered from the user's browser draft. The 16-cell overview is a coarse summary; the exact room plan drives its generated geometry.

The former 50 × 50 ft version remains a separate sample named **My Desired Home · original 50 × 50 ft**. The expanded option requires more land; it is not represented as fitting the original plot.

A **roughly 10 ft right parking lane** stays beside the drawing room, ending at the veranda. The care garden and veranda reach the right boundary, and the southwest bedroom reaches the left boundary. Furniture routes, ventilation, boundary permissions and structural details remain a residential design concept for detailed review, not a clinically specified ICU.

The project selector preserves the current working copy before switching. **Save project** saves on this device and downloads a JSON copy; browser-local saves are not cloud backups. Existing samples remain unchanged. Switching views does not regenerate or modify a plan. A cell overview is approximate and can omit small rooms; the exact editor and drawings contain every room. Editing the cells changes the generation input only when you paint/apply them.
