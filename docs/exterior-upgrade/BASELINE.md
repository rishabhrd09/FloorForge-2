# Baseline and preservation notes

The recommended first slice is the existing 40 × 60 ft, G+1, three-bedroom generator with the road on local -Y. Its room polygons, floor count, aligned stair core, wall/opening model and upper terrace remain the source of truth.

The upgrade adds exterior geometry at the building boundary:

- a plot-contained porch/threshold and, for G+1, a real balcony slab and railing;
- family-specific pergola, screen, accent and roof-edge assemblies;
- an arrival path, planting beds, trees/shrubs and gate metadata;
- room-aware opening refinements recorded as before/after edits on the shared openings.

Interior room cells and stair placement are fingerprinted before and after exterior refinement. Interior palette selection changes material assignments in the scene while keeping procedural furniture/plant placement deterministic.

The compact and small presets retain their existing programme-screen limitations. Exterior geometry does not silently repair an infeasible room programme; the review returns the pre-existing geometry errors for those inputs.
