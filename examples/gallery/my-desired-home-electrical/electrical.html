Inside the wires

Follow the supply from the sealed meter into the main distribution board, through protective devices and concealed conduits in the walls and slabs, to the switchboards, lights, fans, sockets and water heaters of this house. Open a board to see its terminals; click any wire to see what it does.

          Technical sources
            Electrical design boundary. This edition is an educational planning simulation on a copy of the architectural sample. The project has no sanctioned load, DISCOM connection details, earthing-system record, survey or load schedule. Device ratings, cable sizes, discrimination, voltage drop and earth-resistance values are not designed here. A licensed electrical designer, the DISCOM and the electrical inspector must determine the actual supply, protection, routes and tests before any work. Opening a real energised board or meter is dangerous and is not part of this simulation.
