# The Bungalow

South road/front · west carport and side garden · east service lane · north rear garden

A large 100 × 130 ft G+1 government bungalow recreated from the owner's photographs: red comb-frieze parapets, a grilled entrance verandah beside a corrugated carport, a grand drawing hall and dining hall in teak and cane, four bedrooms, ceiling fans and 3.6 m ceilings, a big clipped lawn, rose-covered fences, neem, mango and Ashoka trees.

## Measurements

Plot: 100 × 130 ft (30.480 × 39.624 m). Setbacks: front 45 ft, rear 20 ft, west 25 ft, east 10 ft. Buildable envelope and house footprint: 65 × 65 ft (19.812 × 19.812 m).
Walls: 230 mm brick. Floor-to-floor height: 3.6 m (clear ceilings about 3.44 m). Plinth: 600 mm, reached by Kota-stone steps at every outside door. Ground + first floor, with a roof terrace reached by the stair headhouse.

## The house

- **Facade.** Blush lime render with a red comb frieze (a raised red coping with stripes hanging from it) on every parapet, the terraces and the stair headhouse; a red-oxide plinth; white PVC rain-water pipes with heads.
- **Windows.** Steel windows with small panes and a top ventilator behind dark brick-bond grilles on the ground floor, sloping sheet sunshades on angle brackets, printed cotton curtains on brass rods inside. Wet rooms have obscured glass.
- **Entrance verandah.** Open brick-bond steel grille bays over a mint-green dado, a grille entrance door with a steel kick plate and tower bolt, a side grille door to the carport and paired teak doors with mesh shutters into the drawing hall.
- **Rooms.** A 7.2 × 6.5 m drawing hall and a 7.2 × 5.1 m dining hall in white marble with a dark inlay band, joined by segmental arches to the stair hall; distemper-painted walls (sky blue, pista, peach, lilac, mint, cream, powder pink), moulded cornices, teak door architraves, ceiling fans, a brass chandelier and lanterns, tube lights in the bedrooms.
- **Furniture.** Slatted teak sofa sets and a diwan with bolsters, a glass-fronted showcase, a radio table, a teak dining table with cane chairs, a single-door refrigerator, teak double beds with bedspreads, Godrej-style steel almirahs, dressing tables, aaram-kursi easy chairs, an officer's desk, a cane lounge with a carrom board, a jhoola on the rear verandah, a clay matka, monobloc chairs and moodhas, a charpai and clotheslines on the terraces.
- **Outside.** A corrugated-sheet carport on banded red and black posts, a sheet canopy over the east service lane, a bamboo chick pergola on the front terrace, a water tank and dish on the headhouse, desert coolers on bracket stands.
- **Garden.** Hexagonal paver drive and paths edged with a painted yellow band, rows of red and pink pots and tiered plant stands, a large hedged lawn with monobloc chairs under a great neem, rose and lily beds with saw-tooth brick edging, rose-covered mesh fences, Ashoka spires at the gate, mango and Araucaria trees, a kitchen garden, a tulsi chaura, a garden swing and a rendered compound wall carrying green shade net between red gate pillars with globe lamps.
