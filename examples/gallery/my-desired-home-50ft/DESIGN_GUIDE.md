# My Desired Home

East road/front · south left · north right · west rear

East-facing 50 × 50 ft concept. Front 8 ft; south 5 ft; north 10 ft; zero rear setback is a user-requested concept subject to local approval. The building extends to the rear boundary. The larger drawing room is 4.018 × 3.300 m; the master bedroom is 4.150 × 3.802 m; the home care room has 6.068 × 3.500 m overall extents with a rear service recess. The caregiver bedroom narrows to 3.000 m, with an attached toilet and direct care-room connection. Rear windows are omitted; the caregiver bedroom receives north daylight, and internal toilets need mechanical ventilation design. The common bathroom returns to the rear service zone between the bedroom and care suite, accessed from the living hall, with a full-height ceiling. The former care-supplies room joins that bathroom; the care-room cupboard remains. The staircase is completely clear beneath both flights. A 1.65 m serving counter, 0.40 m deep and 0.95 m high, forms a half divider between kitchen and dining; the remaining 1.65 m stays open for walking. One centered recliner faces the north-wall TV, with two chairs, equipment table and left-hand cupboard. The veranda opens to the right. A new dining glass door opens to a tiled front court. Open kitchen, dining, living and stairs remain connected; the kitchen/stair partition remains. The upstairs lobby overlooks the guarded living void. Two occupied floors and a walkable roof terrace. Materials and lighting remain unchanged. Room dimensions for L shapes are overall extents; areas follow their actual polygons. This is a residential care-room illustration, not a clinically specified ICU. Local setbacks, ventilation, drainage, structure and access require qualified review.
